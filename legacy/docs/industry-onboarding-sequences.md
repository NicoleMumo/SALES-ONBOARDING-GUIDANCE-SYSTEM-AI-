# Industry Onboarding Sequences

## Purpose

Define the industries supported by the prototype and document reference
onboarding fields and processes.

These are reference definitions only. They are not the final Transformer
training data. They provide a starting point for understanding real
onboarding processes and creating training data later.

The Transformer will learn onboarding patterns from collected onboarding
sessions rather than being restricted to these exact processes.

## 1. Banking

### Reference Information

The Central Bank of Kenya's Customer Due Diligence guidance identifies
customer identification, identity verification, beneficial-owner
information where applicable, and information about the purpose and
intended nature of the business relationship as part of customer due
diligence.

### Reference Process

1. Identify the customer.
2. Verify the customer's identity.
3. Identify and verify the beneficial owner where applicable.
4. Obtain information about the purpose and intended nature of the
   business relationship.
5. Continue due diligence based on the customer's relationship and
   risk profile.

This is a simplified reference process. It does not represent the
exact onboarding process of every bank.

## 2. Telecommunications

### Reference Fields

The Kenya Information and Communications (Registration of
Telecommunications Service Subscribers) Regulations, 2025 require
registration information and identification documents.

Examples include:

- Full name
- Identification document information
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Physical address
- Postal address, where applicable
- Other required subscriber information

### Reference Process

1. Collect the subscriber's registration information.
2. Collect the required identification documents.
3. Verify the provided information.
4. Enter or update the verified information in the registration system.
5. Complete subscriber registration.

This is a simplified reference process based on the regulations. It does
not represent the exact workflow of every telecommunications operator.
